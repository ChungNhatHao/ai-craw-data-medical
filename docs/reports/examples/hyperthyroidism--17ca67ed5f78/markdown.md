# Hyperthyroidism

E05 * E05.0 * E05.1 * E05.2 * E05.3 * E05.4 * E05.8 * E05.9

Thyrotoxicosis * Graves' disease * Basedow's disease

Overactivity of the thyroid gland (Graves' disease) resulting in excessive secretion of thyroid hormones. The disease can originate from outside the thyroid.

| Column 1 | Column 2 |
| --- | --- |
| Causes | Autoimmune disorders (Graves' disease / Basedow's disease), inflammation of the gland (thyroiditis), toxic adenoma, carcinoma of the thyroid, hypothalamic-pituitary disease |
| Symptoms | Goitre, abnormal basal metabolic rate, anxiety, nervous irritability, sweating, tremor, tachycardia, palpitations, exophthalmos, weight loss. / Graves' disease / Basedow's disease: Graves' ophthalmopathy, infiltrative dermopathy (localized or pretibial myxedema). |
| Complications | Thyrotoxic coma/crisis with acute hyperthyroidism, cardiac arrhythmias (atrial fibrillation). |
| Treatment | Medication, surgical removal of gland, radioiodine therapy. |
| Prognosis | Mostly good; poor with thyrotoxic crisis. |
| Disability | May occur, depending on severity of symptoms and complications. |
| Supportive evidence | Doctor’s report / T3 / T4 and TSH measurements |
