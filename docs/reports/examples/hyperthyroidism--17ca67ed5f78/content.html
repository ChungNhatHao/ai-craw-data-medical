Hyperthyroidism

Thyrotoxicosis * Graves' disease * Basedow's disease

Overactivity of the thyroid gland (Graves' disease) resulting in excessive secretion of thyroid hormones. The disease can originate from outside the thyroid.

Causes

Autoimmune disorders (Graves' disease / Basedow's disease), inflammation of the gland (thyroiditis), toxic adenoma, carcinoma of the thyroid, hypothalamic-pituitary disease

Symptoms

Goitre, abnormal basal metabolic rate, anxiety, nervous irritability, sweating, tremor, tachycardia, palpitations, exophthalmos, weight loss.

Graves' disease / Basedow's disease: Graves' ophthalmopathy, infiltrative dermopathy (localized or pretibial myxedema).

Complications

Thyrotoxic coma/crisis with acute hyperthyroidism, cardiac arrhythmias (atrial fibrillation).

Treatment

Medication, surgical removal of gland, radioiodine therapy.

Prognosis

Mostly good; poor with thyrotoxic crisis.

Disability

May occur, depending on severity of symptoms and complications.

Supportive evidence

Doctor’s report

T3 / T4 and TSH measurements
